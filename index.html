About / How it works
What is World Cup Map?

A live map showing how fans from every nation are predicting the 2026 World Cup. No account needed, just pick and submit.

How do the map colours work?

Each country is coloured by the team its fans back most to win the tournament. Each country takes the colour of whichever team has the most picks from that nation.

How is match accuracy calculated?

After each match result is confirmed, every prediction is scored. The accuracy leaderboard shows which nations are best at calling results.

Privacy
